Training Videos
in the mobile app

Course → Section → Videos and a section quiz. The course for the app you are signed into comes first; everything else sits under Other Courses.

  1. Open ☰ → My Account → Training Videos.
  2. Continue Watching or open a section from the featured course.
  3. Videos play on automatically; the section quiz unlocks after all videos.
  4. Switch the signed-in app in the menu to see other themes.
